Radiators too

Hi, I'm John*

I've been fitting bathrooms since 2018, along with radiators throughout people's homes.

For me, a bathroom should be the calmest room in the house: somewhere to switch off, run a bath and leave the day at the door. That's what I try to create every time, with careful work, clear prices and no drama along the way.

*I'm the person who'll answer your enquiry, turn up on the day and leave the place tidy afterwards.

Refresh or full install?

Both are priced by written quote, so you know exactly what's included before anything starts.

Bathroom refresh

Keep the layout, change the feel.

  • New taps, basin or toilet
  • Regrouting and resealing
  • A new shower or shower screen
  • Small tiling repairs

Full installation

Start fresh, done properly.

  • Planning the layout with you
  • Taking out the old suite
  • New pipework, suite and tiling
  • A walk-through together before you pay
