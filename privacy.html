How we look after your information

Last updated 20th November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Grout & About, a plumbing business in Hackney, London. If you have a question about your information, email hello.groutandabout@gmail.com

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect your name, email address, phone number, postcode, the job type and description you give us, any estimate you worked out on our calculator, and any photos or videos you choose to send.

Why we collect it

We use it to reply to your enquiry, to arrange a visit and to give you a price. The law lets us do this because you've asked us to, before any agreement to do the work.

Where it's kept

Your enquiry is emailed to us and saved in Google Sheets, and any photos or videos are saved in Google Drive. Google may store this information outside the UK. We look up your postcode with postcodes.io to see which area you're in; only the postcode is sent.

How long we keep it

If we don't go on to do any work for you, we delete your enquiry after 12 months. If we do, we keep it with our job records for two years.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello.groutandabout@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
