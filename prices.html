How we charge, in full
  • Every visit starts at £85, which covers your first hour of work.
  • After that it's £40 for each half hour, charged in half-hour blocks.
  • Parts are charged at cost, with the receipts on your invoice.
  • Travel is included in Dalston, De Beauvoir, Haggerston and London Fields. It's £30 up to a 20-minute bike ride and £50 further out. The map below shows which is which.
  • VAT is included in all our prices and quotes.

How bathroom projects work

Every project gets a free site visit and a written quote. Nothing starts until you've seen it in writing and said yes.

  1. 1

    Tell us about it

    Send an enquiry describing the bathroom you have and the one you want.

  2. 2

    Site visit

    We measure up and talk it through. Site visits are free.

  3. 3

    Written quote

    The scope, what's included, what you supply, the timings and the payment stages.

  4. 4

    Agree and book

    Accept the quote in writing and we fix a start date.

  5. 5

    Pay in stages

    A deposit to book, then a payment as each stage is finished. The stages and amounts are agreed before work starts.

  6. 6

    Finish and sign off

    A walk-through together before the final payment.
